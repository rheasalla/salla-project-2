School levels

A place to grow at every stage.

KCC serves learners across multiple levels, each with its own pathway and support.

  • Tertiary. Degree programs in business, IT, accountancy, education, tourism, psychology, philosophy, and criminology.
  • Grades 11–12. Academic strands including STEM, ABM, HUMSS, and GAS, plus TVL options.
  • K to Grade 10. Elementary and junior high school formation with strong Catholic foundations.
