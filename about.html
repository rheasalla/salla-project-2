Who we are

A close-knit college with a wide horizon.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a faith-centered academic community that welcomes students into a culture of belonging, discipline, and possibility.

Here, students are encouraged to ask good questions, build useful skills, and look beyond themselves. Faculty and staff work together to create a learning experience that is personal, practical, and grounded in service.

“We form people who can meet the world with competence, compassion, and courage.”
What we value

A formation journey that stays with you.

Our values are not just words on a wall. They show up in the way we teach, learn, decide, and care.

01 / Faith

Rooted and reflective.

We make space for conscience, prayer, reflection, and the quiet work of discovering a life of meaning.

02 / Excellence

Prepared and capable.

We nurture high standards, strong study habits, and the confidence to apply learning in the real world.

03 / Service

Generous and responsible.

We challenge students to use what they know to build up families, organizations, and communities.

How we show up

A community that walks with you.

  • Faculty who see students as people, not just performance.
  • Learning experiences that connect theory to community life.
  • Student activities that build friendships, leadership, and confidence.
  • A shared commitment to faith, excellence, and service.
